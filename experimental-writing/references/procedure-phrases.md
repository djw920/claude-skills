# Procedure Phrases — Standard Language Bank

## 1. Reaction Setup

### Addition of reagent to substrate
- "To a stirred solution of **X** ([mass], [mmol], [equiv]) in [solvent] ([vol] mL) at [temp] was added **Y** ([mass/vol], [mmol], [equiv]) dropwise over [time]."
- "A solution of **X** ([mass], [mmol], [equiv]) in [solvent] ([vol] mL) was treated with **Y** ([mass], [mmol], [equiv]) at [temp]."
- "**Y** ([mass], [mmol], [equiv]) was added portionwise to a stirred suspension of **X** ([mass], [mmol], [equiv]) in [solvent] ([vol] mL) at [temp]."

### Cannula / syringe transfer
- "A solution of **Y** in [solvent] ([vol] mL) was transferred via cannula to the above solution at [temp]."

### Sequential additions
- "The mixture was then treated with **Z** ([mass], [mmol], [equiv]), and the resulting mixture was stirred at [temp] for [time]."

### Anhydrous / inert atmosphere conditions
- "under an atmosphere of nitrogen (or argon)"
- "in a flame-dried round-bottomed flask"
- "anhydrous [solvent]"
- "previously dried over 4 Å molecular sieves"

---

## 2. Monitoring

- "The reaction was monitored by TLC (*R*f = [value], [eluent])."
- "Progress was monitored by LC–MS."
- "Completion was determined by TLC analysis (disappearance of starting material spot)."
- "The reaction was stirred for [time] and then judged complete by TLC."

---

## 3. Quench

- "The reaction was quenched by slow addition of saturated aqueous NH₄Cl ([vol] mL)."
- "The reaction was quenched with water ([vol] mL) at 0 °C."
- "The mixture was poured onto ice ([mass] g) and stirred for [time]."
- "The reaction was quenched by addition of 1 N HCl ([vol] mL) at 0 °C."
- "Excess reagent was quenched by careful addition of saturated aqueous Na₂S₂O₃ ([vol] mL)."

---

## 4. Workup — Standard Sequence

"The layers were separated, and the aqueous phase was extracted with [solvent] (3 × [vol] mL). The combined organic extracts were washed sequentially with [saturated aqueous NaHCO₃ / 1 N HCl / brine] ([vol] mL each), dried over [anhydrous MgSO₄ / Na₂SO₄], filtered, and concentrated under reduced pressure."

### Variations
- "The organic layer was dried over anhydrous MgSO₄, filtered through Celite, and concentrated under reduced pressure."
- "The solvent was removed under reduced pressure, and the residue was partitioned between EtOAc ([vol] mL) and water ([vol] mL)."

---

## 5. Purification

### Flash chromatography
- "Purification by flash chromatography on silica gel (0→30% EtOAc/hexanes) afforded **X** as a [color] [form] ([mass] mg, [mmol] mmol, [%])."
- "The crude product was purified by flash chromatography on silica gel (CH₂Cl₂/MeOH, 95:5, v/v) to give **X** ([mass] mg, [%])."

### Preparative HPLC
- "Purification by preparative reverse-phase HPLC (C18 column, 10→90% MeCN/H₂O + 0.1% TFA, 20 min gradient) afforded **X** as a white solid after lyophilization ([mass] mg, [%])."

### Recrystallization
- "The solid residue was recrystallized from [EtOH/H₂O] to afford **X** as colorless needles ([mass] mg, [%])."

### Trituration
- "The crude solid was triturated with cold [Et₂O/hexanes] ([vol] mL), collected by filtration, and dried under vacuum to give **X** ([mass] mg, [%])."

---

## 6. Physical Forms

| Form | Correct phrasing |
|------|-----------------|
| Clear, colorless | colorless oil |
| Pale yellow liquid | pale yellow oil |
| White crystalline | white solid |
| Off-white powder | off-white solid |
| Amorphous | amorphous white solid |
| Foamy | white foam |
| Sticky, viscous | yellow gum |
| Glassy, brittle | colorless glass |

Avoid "waxy solid" or "semi-solid" unless truly unavoidable.

---

## 7. Special Conditions

### Low temperature
- "at −78 °C (dry ice/acetone bath)"
- "at 0 °C (ice/water bath)"
- "The mixture was allowed to warm gradually to ambient temperature over 2 h."

### Concentration-sensitive reactions
- "The reaction was performed at high dilution (0.01 m) to suppress intermolecular pathways."
- "The reaction mixture was added dropwise via syringe pump over 4 h to a refluxing solution of..."

### Instability notes
- "The product is sensitive to moisture and was stored under argon at −20 °C."
- "The product decomposes slowly on silica gel and was used immediately after isolation."
- "Spectroscopic data were acquired within 24 h of isolation."

---

## 8. Known Compound Entry

"Compound **X** was prepared according to the reported procedure of [Author et al., ref] with minor modification (see SI for details) and obtained in [%] yield. Spectral data were in agreement with those reported."

If significant modification:
"Compound **X** was prepared by adaptation of the procedure of [Author et al., ref]; yield [%]. ¹H NMR (400 MHz, CDCl₃) δ [key signals consistent with literature]."

---

## 9. General Procedure Reference

When citing a general procedure already defined in the Experimental Section:
"Prepared according to General Procedure A from **substrate Y** ([mass], [mmol])."

Do not re-specify all conditions — they belong in the General Procedure definition.
